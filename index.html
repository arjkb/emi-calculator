    Comparison

    Interest is counted from today onwards. What you have already paid cannot be worked out from a balance alone, so it is left out. Front-loaded counts only your extra payments. It is money paid earlier, not money paid on top, which is why Total outlay goes down as it goes up.

    Monthly reducing balance. The monthly rate is the yearly rate divided by twelve, the way Indian lenders work it out. EMIs are whole rupees, rounded up so the loan finishes within its tenure. The last one is smaller and clears whatever is left.
